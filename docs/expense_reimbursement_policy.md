# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-POL-007
**Effective date:** 2025-02-01
**Owner:** Finance

## 1. General Principle
Employees will be reimbursed for reasonable, business-related expenses
incurred while performing their job duties, provided they are submitted with
a valid receipt within 60 days of the expense date.

## 2. Submission Process
1. Log the expense in the Expense Portal with a scanned or photographed
   receipt.
2. Select the correct cost center and expense category.
3. Submit for manager approval.
4. Finance processes approved reimbursements within 10 business days via
   the employee's standard payroll method.

## 3. Travel Expenses
- Economy class flights are covered for trips under 6 hours; premium economy
  is covered for flights over 6 hours.
- Hotel accommodation is capped at $250/night in major metro areas and
  $150/night elsewhere, unless pre-approved otherwise.
- Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $75/day without itemized
  receipts required under $25 per meal.

## 4. Client Entertainment
Client meals and entertainment require pre-approval from a Director-level
manager or above and must include the names of all attendees.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Items
- Alcohol for personal consumption
- Traffic fines and parking tickets incurred through negligence
- Personal entertainment (movies, minibar, spa services)
- First-class airfare

## 6. Corporate Card
Employees with a corporate card should use it for all eligible business
expenses instead of paying out of pocket. Corporate card statements must be
reconciled monthly in the Expense Portal.

## 7. Questions
Contact finance-help@company.example for clarification on specific expense
categories.
