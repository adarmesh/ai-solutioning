# Leave and Time Off Policy (United States Employees)

**Document ID:** HR-POL-014
**Effective date:** 2025-01-01
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines paid time off (PTO), sick leave, and holiday entitlements for
full-time employees based in the United States.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Full-time employees accrue 1.75 PTO days per month, totaling 21 days per year.
- PTO accrual begins on the employee's start date. There is no waiting period.
- Unused PTO may be carried over into the next calendar year up to a maximum of
  5 days. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31st.
- PTO requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 business days in
  advance for requests longer than 2 days.

## 3. Sick Leave
- Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, separate from PTO.
- Sick leave does not carry over to the following year.
- A doctor's note is required for sick leave lasting longer than 3 consecutive
  days.

## 4. Parental Leave
- Primary caregivers are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave.
- Secondary caregivers are eligible for 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
- Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption date.
- This policy applies only to US-based employees. Employees based outside the
  US should refer to their local country addendum (not included in this
  corpus).

## 5. Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year, published annually by
People Operations in December for the following calendar year.

## 6. Approval Process
1. Employee submits a leave request through the HR portal.
2. Direct manager approves or rejects within 3 business days.
3. People Operations is notified automatically once approved.
