# New Employee Onboarding Procedure

**Document ID:** OPS-PROC-002
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Before Day One
- People Operations sends the offer letter and new-hire paperwork via the
  HR portal at least 1 week before the start date.
- IT provisions a company laptop, email account, and default access
  (email, chat, HR portal, expense portal) 3 business days before start.
- Manager assigns an onboarding buddy from the new hire's team.

## 2. Day One
1. New hire completes I-9/tax paperwork (US) or local equivalent in the HR
   portal.
2. IT Service Desk walks through laptop setup, MFA enrollment (see
   Information Security Policy Section 2), and VPN setup (see VPN Setup
   Guide).
3. Manager holds a welcome meeting covering team structure, current
   projects, and first-week goals.

## 3. First Week
- Complete mandatory compliance training: Information Security Awareness,
  Code of Conduct, and (for engineering roles) Secure Coding Practices.
- Meet with onboarding buddy at least twice.
- Gain access to team-specific systems requested by the manager through
  the IT Service Desk.

## 4. 30/60/90 Check-ins
- **30 days:** Manager and new hire review onboarding feedback and set
  goals for the next 60 days.
- **60 days:** People Operations sends an engagement survey.
- **90 days:** Formal end of the probationary period; manager submits a
  confirmation form to People Operations.

## 5. Equipment and Access Offboarding Note
Any equipment issued during onboarding must be returned per the standard
offboarding checklist if employment ends before the equipment is fully
depreciated (see Finance's asset management process, not included in this
corpus).
