# Remote Work and Hybrid Schedule Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-022
**Effective date:** 2024-06-01
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Eligibility
Employees in good standing (not currently on a performance improvement plan)
may work remotely subject to manager approval and role requirements. Roles
requiring physical presence (e.g., lab technicians, on-site support) are not
eligible.

## 2. Hybrid Schedule
- Default company schedule is 3 days in-office (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday)
  and 2 days remote (Monday, Friday).
- Teams may request an alternate schedule from their department head.

## 3. Fully Remote Arrangements
- Employees may request a fully remote arrangement after 6 months of tenure.
- Fully remote employees must be available during core hours: 10:00-16:00 in
  their team's primary timezone.
- Fully remote employees must attend in-person team offsites at least twice
  per year, travel funded by the company per the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## 4. Equipment
- The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home office
  stipend for remote employees.
- Equipment requests are submitted via the IT Service Desk.

## 5. International Remote Work
Working remotely from a country other than the employee's country of
employment for more than 20 consecutive days requires prior approval from
Legal and People Operations due to tax and employment law implications.

## 6. Review
This policy is reviewed annually by the Executive Team each January.
